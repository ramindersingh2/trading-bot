plans.jsonl — What Goes In

  • • id + timestamp of every run
  • • LLM's market thesis (its reasoning)
  • • What research it did (googled what)
  • • Full step-by-step plan text
  • • Action taken: open/close/hold
  • • Confidence level + P&L at that moment
  • • NEVER deleted — your full audit trail

trades.jsonl — What Goes In

  • • One entry when trade is OPENED
  • • Same entry UPDATED when trade CLOSED
  • • Symbol, strike, expiry, qty, prices
  • • Tradier order ID (links to real order)
  • • plan_id (links to which plan opened it)
  • • Final P&L when closed
  • • NEVER deleted — every trade ever made

What LLM Gets Next Run

  • • Portfolio: cash, total P&L, win rate
  • • Open positions: full detail with live P&L
  • • Closed trades: summary only (count + P&L)
  • • Last 15 days plans: full detail
  • • Older plans: NOT sent (keeps prompt lean)
  • • Fresh market data + news every single run

Why This Design Works

  • • LLM sees consequences of its own decisions
  • • "I opened a call, it lost $200 — why?"
  • • Builds its OWN pattern recognition over time
  • • Prompt stays lean — no token overload
  • • You can inspect every decision ever made
  • • Full audit trail if anything goes wrong
